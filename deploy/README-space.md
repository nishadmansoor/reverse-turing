# Reverse Turing Test

Three independent classifiers judge whether a piece of text was written by a
human or generated by a language model, and a stacked meta-classifier weighs
their votes.

- **Transformer** — fine-tuned DistilBERT reading the text as language
- **Stylometric** — logistic regression over sentence rhythm, vocabulary
  richness, and punctuation density
- **Visual CNN** — renders the text as a heatmap image and classifies the
  texture of its structure

Trained on [RAID](https://huggingface.co/datasets/liamdugan/raid) — 156k samples
spanning many modern generators. All reported figures are measured on a held-out
test split; see the **Method & Results** tab in the app for the numbers and the
known failure modes.

Predictions are probabilistic. This is a demonstration of ensemble methods, not
an authorship-verification tool, and it should not be used to accuse anyone of
anything.

Source: https://github.com/nishadmansoor/reverse-turing
