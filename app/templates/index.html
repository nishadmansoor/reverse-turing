Every sample is a real row from the held-out RAID test split, so the badge is verified ground truth and no model saw it during training. The two research abstracts share a subject on purpose — one human, one generated — since telling those apart cannot be done on topic alone.

How it works

Three independent classifiers each read the text, and a fourth model — a stacked meta-classifier fitted on held-out data — decides how much to trust each one.

  1. Transformer — a fine-tuned DistilBERT reading the text as language.
  2. Stylometric — logistic regression over sentence rhythm, vocabulary richness, and punctuation.
  3. Visual CNN — renders the text as a heatmap image and classifies the texture of its structure.

The interesting part is where they disagree. Short text starves the stylometric features; heavily edited text confuses the transformer. The method page reports where each one fails.
